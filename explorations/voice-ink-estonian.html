Voice into ink, Byro-short.

What you like about byro.ee, turned into a brief, then crossed with Direction H. Each name gets its own version of the voice-into-ink move, and every time the ink line becomes part of the name's letters, so mark and word are one idea.

Short4–5 letters, one or two syllables.
Soft and lowercaseFriendly, not corporate, not literal.
A real rootBuilt from an Estonian word, sounds global.
Voice into inkSound becomes a handwritten line, landing on an orange dot.

Deliberately not Byro: byro.ee is a direct competitor (a "reputation workspace" turning company knowledge into LinkedIn content, built in Estonia). So no forest green and no bird mascot here. The palette is ink, paper and Social Catalyst's orange, which you already own.

My pick: juttu

juttu is the strongest of the five. It means talk and story in Estonian, and "a thing, a story, a joke" in Finnish, so it lands across the Nordics; it is warm, says itself the way it is spelled, and juttu.ee, juttu.ai and getjuttu.com all came back free. Its mark is the best fusion with Direction H: the sound wave writes the j, and the j's dot is the orange ink drop, so the logo is the letter and the letter is the idea.

Runner-up: kuula ("listen!"), the clearest product story, if you prefer meaning over warmth.

Before deciding: a quick check with a native Estonian speaker on connotations, then a trademark knockout in the EU. Domain checks were live on 5 October 2026; "free" means no DNS record, not guaranteed for sale.
