Say it: TELL-zee. "Tell" with a soft, friendly ending, like Etsy.

The move: the two l's are the sound bars (they pulse like a voice), and the y's tail runs on as an ink line to the orange dot. The name is the waveform.

Icon: just the two bars and the dot. Reads at favicon size.

Free: tellsy.ai · tellsy.app · tellsy.co · gettellsy.com · tellsyhq.com
