My shortlist

Not simply the top five scores: the best mix of score, a free domain variant, easy pronunciation, and a story you can tell in one sentence. Lafz and Bayaan score as well, but are harder to say on first read.

How to choose

If the company is global B2B first, go with Heard. It is the outcome every founder wants, one syllable, a real word with emotional pull, and getheard.com came back free. Its weakness is trademark strength (common words are hard to protect), so pair it with a distinctive wordmark.

If your story is the brand, go with Awaaz. It means voice, English speakers can say it on first read, and it carries the Pakistani-founder-in-Tallinn story without needing explanation beyond "it means voice". getawaaz.com and awaazhq.com came back free.

Foreword is the most investor-credible: editorial, and it sounds like "forward", so it carries momentum as well as writing. foreword.app came back free.

Next step for whichever two you like: a trademark knockout search in the EU, UK and US, buy the domains the same day, then a designer takes the wordmark from concept to final.
